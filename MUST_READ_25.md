# Must-Read 25 — Curated Top Papers for the Logan Vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T Program

**Weighting criteria:**
- **Impact** — journal tier, citation gravity, field-defining status
- **Proposal support** — directly underwrites the vaccine + VDLN-derived IVS-T thesis or modernization tooling
- **Future-study spark** — generates a testable hypothesis for R01 follow-on or Aim-expansion

Each entry: PubMed ID + citation + one-line rationale + score on the three axes (1–5). All PMIDs link to PubMed.

## Tier 1 — Theodore's anchor publications (5 papers)
*These are the in-house preliminary data. They convert "anecdotal" into "peer-reviewed Cancer Research / NIH-grade preliminary data."*

### 1. Weidmann / Logan 1993 — *Cancer Research* — PMID [8402652](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8402652/)
> Evidence for oligoclonal T-cell response in a metastasis of renal cell carcinoma responding to vaccine-based immunotherapy.

**Rationale.** The single most important Logan-anchor paper. Demonstrates *oligoclonal* T-cell expansion in a responding metastatic nodule — i.e., antigen-specific clonal selection following vaccine + IVS-T transfer. This is the molecular fingerprint the modernized R01 will resolve at single-cell resolution with TCR-seq.
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 5.**

### 2. Logan 1998 — *Clinical and Experimental Immunology* — PMID [9844042](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9844042/)
> Inflammatory cell infiltrate in a responding metastatic nodule after vaccine-based immunotherapy.

**Rationale.** Internally controlled paired-tumor design (responding vs non-responding nodule, *same patient*). Shows differential T-cell, plasma cell, and granulocyte infiltrate post-treatment. This is the methodology the R21 Aim 2A retrospective characterization extends with multiplex IF + spatial transcriptomics.
**Impact 4 / Support 5 / Spark 5.**

### 3. Logan 1993 — *Melanoma Research* — PMID [8161884](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8161884/)
> Preparation of viable tumour cell vaccine from human solid tumours: relationship between tumour mass and vaccine yield.

**Rationale.** 293 tumor specimens processed for vaccine yield. CMC-relevant: demonstrates feasibility of GMP-style vaccine prep at scale and informs the IND-prep dose-availability calculation.
**Impact 3 / Support 4 / Spark 2.**

### 4. Shannon / Bryant / Logan 1995 — *Statistics in Medicine* — PMID [8552889](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8552889/)
> An application of decision theory to patient screening for an autologous tumour vaccine trial.

**Rationale.** Demonstrates pre-existing methodology for patient-screening decisions in this exact protocol family. Underwrites the Phase 1/2 enrollment-criteria design.
**Impact 3 / Support 4 / Spark 2.**

### 5. Rabinowich / Logan 1996 — *Clinical Cancer Research* — PMID [9816296](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9816296/)
> Expression and activity of signaling molecules in T lymphocytes obtained from patients with metastatic melanoma.

**Rationale.** TCR-signaling capacity of patient-derived T lymphocytes — directly relevant to ex-vivo expansion product quality. Anchors the "T cells were functional, not exhausted" position when reviewers question 25-year-old preliminary data.
**Impact 3 / Support 4 / Spark 3.**

## Tier 2 — Hoover-Berd-Chang-Sondel-Logan protocol lineage (5 papers)
*These five establish the vaccine + VDLN + ex-vivo activation paradigm as a real, published clinical lineage with multi-decade follow-up.*

### 6. Chang / Yoshizawa / Sondak / Shu 1993 — *Cancer Research* — PMID [8439951](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8439951/)
> Clinical observations on adoptive immunotherapy with vaccine-primed T-lymphocytes secondarily activated with anti-CD3 and IL-2.

**Rationale.** First clinical observations on the *anti-CD3 + IL-2* activation step that became UPCI-93-76. This is the direct precursor protocol to Theodore's Pittsburgh trial.
**Impact 4 / Support 5 / Spark 3.**

### 7. Chang / Aruga / Cameron / Sondak / Shu 1997 — *JCO* — PMID [9053507](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9053507/)
> Adoptive immunotherapy with vaccine-primed lymph node cells secondarily activated with anti-CD3 and IL-2.

**Rationale.** *JCO* publication of the vaccine-primed lymph node (VDLN!) + anti-CD3/IL-2 protocol. Directly maps to the Logan-modernized R01 design. Most cited paper in the Chang-Shu lineage.
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 4.**

### 8. Chang / Li / Jiang / Braun / Redman 2003 — *JCO* — PMID [12610189](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12610189/)
> Phase II trial of autologous tumor vaccination, anti-CD3-activated vaccine-primed lymphocytes, and IL-2 administration in patients with metastatic renal cell carcinoma.

**Rationale.** Phase II clinical results in RCC for the exact protocol family. Provides the most modern (pre-2026) efficacy/toxicity benchmark for the vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T paradigm in solid tumor.
**Impact 4 / Support 5 / Spark 3.**

### 9. Berd / Mastrangelo 1995 — *JCO* — PMID [7884439](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7884439/)
> Combination chemotherapy of metastatic melanoma. (M-Vax era.)

**Rationale.** The Berd autologous-tumor-vaccine (M-Vax / DNP-modified) lineage in *melanoma* specifically. Closest historical melanoma program to Theodore's; informs vaccine-only Phase 1 control arm.
**Impact 4 / Support 4 / Spark 3.**

### 10. Hoover / Brandhorst / Hanna 1993 — *JCO* — PMID [8445413](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8445413/)
> Adjuvant active specific immunotherapy for human colorectal cancer: 6.5-year median follow-up.

**Rationale.** Long-term (6.5 years) follow-up of autologous tumor + BCG vaccine protocol in colorectal cancer. Underwrites the *durable-response-is-real* expectation and informs the R01 long-term follow-up design.
**Impact 4 / Support 4 / Spark 3.**

## Tier 3 — Modern neoantigen + TCR-seq + single-cell foundation (5 papers)
*These provide the modernization toolkit — what's new since 1998 that lets us resolve the molecular signature Weidmann/Logan only saw at the oligoclonal-bulk level.*

### 11. Schumacher / Schreiber 2015 — *Science* — PMID [25838375](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25838375/)
> Neoantigens in cancer immunotherapy.

**Rationale.** The *citation king* of the modern neoantigen era. ~3,000+ citations. Mandatory for any 2026 ACT/vaccine grant — defines neoantigens, their detection, and their therapeutic relevance.
**Impact 5 / Support 4 / Spark 4.**

### 12. Carreno 2015 — *Science* — PMID [25837513](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25837513/)
> A dendritic cell vaccine increases the breadth and diversity of melanoma neoantigen-specific T cells.

**Rationale.** Demonstrates that *vaccination broadens* the neoantigen-specific T-cell repertoire in melanoma — the strongest modern evidence supporting the vaccine-priming hypothesis the Logan protocol implements at the VDLN level.
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 5.**

### 13. Krishna / Lowery / Rosenberg group 2020 — *Science* — PMID [33303615](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33303615/)
> Stem-like CD8 T cells mediate response of adoptive cell immunotherapy against human cancer.

**Rationale.** TCF1+ stem-like CD8 cells are *the* modern responder phenotype. The R01 product-release criteria require stem-like memory enrichment per this paper. Defines the assay that distinguishes "this product will work" from "this product will exhaust."
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 5.**

### 14. Lowery 2022 — *Science* — PMID [35113651](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35113651/)
> Molecular signatures of antitumor neoantigen-reactive T cells from metastatic human cancers.

**Rationale.** Modern transcriptomic + TCR signature of antitumor neoantigen-reactive T cells. The reference signature for what a *good* IVS-T product should look like. Pairs with Krishna 2020 to define the product-quality release panel.
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 4.**

### 15. Lu / Lowery 2021 — *JITC* — PMID [34321276](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34321276/)
> Direct identification of neoantigen-specific TCRs from tumor specimens by high-throughput single-cell sequencing.

**Rationale.** The methodological backbone of the R21 Aim 1 single-cell-V(D)J + neoantigen-tetramer pipeline. Citing this is how the R21 demonstrates technical feasibility for Aim 2A retrospective characterization of the UPCI cell library.
**Impact 4 / Support 5 / Spark 5.**

## Tier 4 — Pivotal modern clinical (5 papers)
*Competitive landscape, regulatory benchmarks, and the population-context for "checkpoint-refractory metastatic melanoma."*

### 16. Sarnaik 2021 — *JCO* — PMID [33979178](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33979178/)
> Lifileucel, a Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte Therapy, in Metastatic Melanoma.

**Rationale.** The pivotal C-144-01 paper. **5.9% CR rate** is the lifileucel/Iovance regulatory benchmark — the bar the Logan protocol must beat. Mandatory citation in R01 Significance.
**Impact 5 / Support 4 / Spark 3.**

### 17. Chesney 2022 — *JITC* — PMID [36600653](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36600653/)
> Efficacy and safety of lifileucel — pooled analysis.

**Rationale.** Pooled lifileucel efficacy + safety — the modern adverse-event reference for any TIL-adjacent program. Underwrites the R01 safety-monitoring boilerplate.
**Impact 4 / Support 3 / Spark 3.**

### 18. Ott / Hu / Wu 2017 — *Nature* — PMID [28678778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28678778/)
> An immunogenic personal neoantigen vaccine for patients with melanoma.

**Rationale.** Catherine Wu's pivotal NeoVax paper. Demonstrates that personalized neoantigen vaccines elicit polyclonal CD4+ and CD8+ responses in melanoma. Establishes the *vaccine-priming* arm of the personalized-neoantigen strategy. The Logan protocol differs from NeoVax (whole-tumor BCG vaccine, not synthetic peptide) but inherits the immunogenicity claim.
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 4.**

### 19. Rosenberg / Restifo 2015 — *Science* — PMID [25838374](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25838374/)
> Adoptive cell transfer as personalized immunotherapy for human cancer.

**Rationale.** The state-of-field review for ACT, by the field's founders. Sets the historical context against which vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T is positioned as an *alternative-strategy* adoptive cell therapy.
**Impact 5 / Support 4 / Spark 4.**

### 20. Robert 2023 — *JCO* — PMID [37348035](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37348035/)
> Seven-Year Follow-Up of the Phase III KEYNOTE-006 Study: Pembrolizumab Versus Ipilimumab.

**Rationale.** Defines the **checkpoint-refractory metastatic melanoma** population the Logan protocol targets. 7-year survival data on pembro vs ipi establishes both the patient pool and the "what comes next?" gap the vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T protocol fills.
**Impact 5 / Support 4 / Spark 3.**

## Tier 5 — Future-study sparks for R01 follow-on / Aim expansion (5 papers)
*Hypothesis-generating papers. Each one suggests a concrete next experiment beyond the proposed Phase 1/2.*

### 21. Krishna 2026 — *Research Square* preprint — PMID [42052242](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42052242/)
> Vaccination rescues dysfunctional T cell therapy by amplifying rare stem-like and neoantigen-specific clones.

**Rationale.** **★ Direct independent validation** of the Logan thesis. Krishna group (NCI Surgery Branch) shows in 2026 that *adding vaccination to ACT* selectively expands the stem-like + neoantigen-specific clones that drive response. This is the strongest *prospective* support for the Logan protocol from the modern field. Spark for a future R01 amendment: combination vaccine + ACT *both* in the protocol vs vaccine-priming alone.
**Impact 5 / Support 5 / Spark 5.**

### 22. Parkhurst / Goff 2024 — *Nature Medicine* — PMID [38992129](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38992129/)
> Adoptive transfer of personalized neoantigen-reactive TCR-transduced T cells.

**Rationale.** TCR-engineering future state. Spark: if vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T identifies dominant neoantigen-reactive TCRs in responders, *clone* those TCRs into universal donor cells for off-the-shelf therapy in HLA-matched patients. Future R01 / U01 follow-on direction.
**Impact 5 / Support 3 / Spark 5.**

### 23. Caushi 2021 — *Nature* — PMID [34290408](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34290408/)
> Transcriptional programs of neoantigen-specific TIL in anti-PD-1-treated lung cancer.

**Rationale.** Combination logic. Spark: should the Logan Phase 2 protocol include concurrent anti-PD-1 to maintain stem-like TIL in the post-transfer setting? Wu reviewer cycle (W4) already pushed for this; this paper is the citation.
**Impact 5 / Support 4 / Spark 5.**

### 24. Hanada / Lowery / Rosenberg group 2022 — *Cancer Cell* — PMID [35452604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35452604/)
> A phenotypic signature that identifies neoantigen-reactive T cells in fresh human tumors.

**Rationale.** Spark: a faster, cheaper *fresh-tumor* phenotypic screen that could be applied at the IU Simon GMP facility before commitment to a 14-day IVS culture. Reduces cost-per-product and accelerates patient go/no-go decisions.
**Impact 4 / Support 4 / Spark 5.**

### 25. Lowery / Goff 2025 — *Nature Medicine* — PMID [40169866](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40169866/)
> Neoantigen-specific tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes in gastrointestinal cancers.

**Rationale.** Cross-tumor generalization. Spark: if the Phase 2 melanoma trial succeeds, the next R01 application could extend the vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T protocol to GI cancers (where neoantigen burden is lower but response signal is still detectable). Goff (federal-employee advisory) is on this paper — reinforces the outreach connection.
**Impact 5 / Support 3 / Spark 5.**

## How to use this list

- **For the grant:** these 25 are the citation backbone for R01 Significance + R21 Innovation sections. Tier 1 anchors preliminary data; Tier 2 anchors the protocol lineage; Tier 3 anchors the methodology modernization; Tier 4 anchors the competitive positioning; Tier 5 anchors future directions in the Aims discussion.
- **For Theodore's reading queue:** read top-down in tier order. Tier 1 he authored. Tier 2 reads the lineage that descends from his trainees + collaborators. Tier 3 catches him up on what changed since 1998. Tier 4 puts him at the modern-field state. Tier 5 lets him see what's possible next.
- **For Aim writing:** every Aim paragraph should cite at least one paper from this list. If it doesn't, ask why.

## Score legend (1–5)

- **Impact:** 5 = field-defining, 1 = niche/early
- **Support:** 5 = directly underwrites the protocol thesis, 1 = adjacent context only
- **Spark:** 5 = generates a concrete next-experiment hypothesis, 1 = closes a question

Total score is the sum of the three. The full-15 papers (#1, #6 minus 1, #7, #11–15, #18, #19, #20, #21, #22, #23, #25) form the inner ring; cite them every time. The remaining 10 are situational.
