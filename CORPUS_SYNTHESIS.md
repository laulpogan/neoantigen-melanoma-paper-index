## Through-line narrative — how we got here, in 8 acts

**Act 1 (1972–1985).** Surgical oncology discovers that tumor cells + adjuvant (BCG, vaccinia) injected back into the patient sometimes provokes regression of distant disease. Hoover, Hanna, Mastrangelo, Wallack publish small adjuvant-immunotherapy series in colorectal and melanoma. Mechanism unknown — the field assumes "specific active immunotherapy" without identifying which T-cell specificities matter.

**Act 2 (1986–1995).** Recombinant IL-2 makes ex-vivo lymphocyte expansion practical. Rosenberg, Lotze, Mazumder demonstrate that lymphokine-activated killer (LAK) cells and tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TIL) can produce objective responses in metastatic melanoma. **In parallel**, Hoover/Berd/Chang/Sondel/Logan develop a different branch: vaccinate the patient with autologous tumor + BCG, surgically harvest the *vaccine-draining lymph node* (VDLN), expand its lymphocytes ex vivo with autologous tumor + IL-2 (or anti-CD3 + IL-2), reinfuse. Theodore runs UPCI-86-13, -92-81, -93-76 in this branch. Weidmann/Logan 1993 (PMID 8402652) shows oligoclonal T-cell expansion in a responding metastasis — the first molecular-level evidence the protocol works.

**Act 3 (1995–2005).** Field shifts from "uncharacterized tumor-specific antigen" to **defined antigens** — MART-1, gp100, tyrosinase, MAGE-A. Tetramer staining and T-cell cloning let researchers track antigen-specific responses. Peptide vaccines proliferate. Limitation recognized at the time: shared antigens are also expressed in normal tissue (tolerance) and cover only a fraction of the patient-specific antigenic landscape.

**Act 4 (2002–2015).** **Lymphodepletion** (Cy/Flu) before TIL transfer dramatically improves engraftment and response durability. Rosenberg group reports CR rates approaching 24% in checkpoint-naive melanoma. The "TIL paradigm" hardens: bulk tumor-derived lymphocytes, lymphodepleting conditioning, high-dose IL-2 support. The vaccine + VDLN branch is largely sidelined as TIL becomes the dominant academic ACT modality.

**Act 5 (2010–2017).** Checkpoint inhibitors (anti-CTLA-4, anti-PD-1) transform metastatic melanoma. Pembrolizumab and nivolumab become first-line. Patients who progress on checkpoint inhibitors define a new clinical population: **checkpoint-refractory metastatic melanoma** — the population the Logan revival targets.

**Act 6 (2013–2020).** **Neoantigen discovery era.** Whole-exome sequencing + MHC-binding prediction (NetMHC, NetMHCpan) make it possible to identify the patient-specific mutated peptides T cells actually recognize. Schumacher/Schreiber 2015 *Science* (PMID 25838375) becomes the citation king. Carreno 2015 *Science* (PMID 25837513) shows DC vaccines broaden the neoantigen-specific repertoire — the first modern evidence supporting vaccine-priming as an enrichment strategy.

**Act 7 (2017–2024).** **Personalized vaccines** (Wu's NeoVax, Moderna's mRNA-4157) and **commercial TIL** (Iovance's lifileucel — FDA approved Feb 2024, 5.9% CR rate). Single-cell V(D)J sequencing and TCR-seq become standard. Krishna 2020 *Science* (PMID 33303615) and Lowery 2022 *Science* (PMID 35113651) define the **TCF1+ stem-like memory** phenotype as the responder signature — a product-release biomarker that did not exist in 1998.

**Act 8 (2025–2026).** Krishna 2026 preprint (PMID 42052242) shows in 2026 that *vaccination rescues dysfunctional ACT* by amplifying rare stem-like and neoantigen-specific clones — direct independent prospective validation of what Theodore's UPCI protocols implicitly did 30 years earlier. Cell library from UPCI-86-13/-92-81/-93-76 is relocated to IU Simon (Nguyen et al. AACR 2025). The pieces are in place to revive the vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T paradigm with modern tooling.

**That's the proposal.** The Logan revival sits at the convergence point: it reactivates a published clinical lineage with peer-reviewed preliminary data and a banked cell library, modernizes it with single-cell TCR-seq + neoantigen-prediction + TCF1+ phenotype panels, and tests it in the checkpoint-refractory population the field acknowledges has no good answer.

## The 20 era buckets — one paragraph each

Each era below has a short narrative, a count, and the 1–2 most-cited or highest-impact papers in the bucket (with PMID linking to PubMed).

### Era 01 — IVS / LAK era (1988–1995) — 200 papers

The opening era of clinical adoptive immunotherapy. Lymphokine-activated killer (LAK) cells and in-vitro-sensitized lymphocytes against autologous tumor lysate, all run with high-dose IL-2 support. Mechanism — which specificities mattered — was unknown at the time. Response rates 10–20%, durability poor. **This is the era you practiced in**; UPCI-86-13 starts here. The Rosenberg / Lotze / Mazumder protocols are the published mainstream; the Hoover / Berd / Chang / Sondel / Logan branch is the published alternative.
- **Top:** PMID [2360981](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2360981/) (Ades 1990) — immune responses during IL-2 + LAK adoptive immunotherapy in humans.
- **Methodological:** PMID [2120581](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2120581/) (Bergmann/Weidmann 1990) — cytokine induction of LAK cells.

### Era 02 — TIL + IL-2 era (1988–2000) — 46 papers

The Rosenberg-group TIL paradigm: extract lymphocytes directly from resected tumor (rather than from VDLN or peripheral blood), expand ex vivo with IL-2, reinfuse. Smaller bucket because the field wasn't yet calling it "TIL therapy" formally; the foundational papers are mostly in era 01. The 46 here are the methodological refinements (TIL expansion protocols, IL-2 dose-finding, early efficacy reports).
- **Top:** Foundational TIL methodology papers from the Rosenberg group; see era 04 for the lymphodepletion-era continuation.

### Era 03 — Defined-antigen era (1995–2005) — 200 papers

The field gets antigen-resolution. MART-1, gp100, tyrosinase, MAGE-A family identified as shared melanoma antigens. Peptide vaccines proliferate (with mostly disappointing clinical results). T-cell cloning and tetramer staining let researchers track antigen-specific T cells in vivo for the first time. Sets the stage for the neoantigen era by demonstrating that shared antigens are too tolerized and too narrow to drive durable response.
- **Top:** Tetramer-staining method papers establishing antigen-specific T-cell tracking.

### Era 04 — ACT + lymphodepletion era (2002–2015) — 193 papers

Lymphodepletion (Cy/Flu) before TIL transfer becomes the dominant paradigm. Rosenberg group reports CR rates ≥20% in checkpoint-naive metastatic melanoma. High-dose IL-2 support. This is the era TIL therapy hardens into the protocol that becomes lifileucel a decade later. The vaccine + VDLN branch loses academic share to the TIL paradigm during this period.
- **Top:** PMID [25838374](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25838374/) — Rosenberg/Restifo 2015 *Science* — adoptive cell transfer review (state-of-field).
- **Brain mets:** PMID [20719934](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20719934/) — Hong/Rosenberg 2010 — successful TIL treatment of melanoma brain metastases.

### Era 05 — Neoantigen-discovery era (2013–2020) — 200 papers

Whole-exome sequencing + MHC-binding prediction make it possible to identify patient-specific mutated peptides T cells actually recognize. The conceptual shift from "shared antigens" to "private mutated antigens" reframes the entire ACT field. Tran shows responding patients have neoantigen-reactive T cells; Lowery, Krishna, Yossef build the modern TCR-discovery pipeline.
- **Top:** PMID [25838375](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25838375/) — Schumacher/Schreiber 2015 *Science* — neoantigens in cancer immunotherapy (citation king of the era).
- **Method:** PMID [33303615](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33303615/) — Krishna 2020 *Science* — stem-like CD8 T cells mediate ACT response.

### Era 06 — Personalized-vaccine era (2017–2026) — 199 papers

NeoVax (Wu, Dana-Farber) and mRNA-4157 (Moderna + Merck, KEYNOTE-942) demonstrate that personalized peptide / mRNA vaccines can elicit polyclonal CD4 + CD8 responses against patient-specific neoantigens in melanoma. Adjuvant-setting trials show recurrence-free survival benefit. The Logan vaccine is conceptually adjacent but uses whole-tumor + BCG (broader antigen presentation) rather than synthetic peptide / mRNA (narrow, pre-selected).
- **Top:** PMID [28678778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28678778/) — Ott/Hu/Wu 2017 *Nature* — NeoVax personal neoantigen vaccine in melanoma.
- **Vaccine + repertoire:** PMID [25837513](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25837513/) — Carreno 2015 *Science* — DC vaccine increases breadth of neoantigen response.

### Era 07 — TIL clinical modern era (2018–2026) — 154 papers

The lifileucel pivotal era. C-144-01 (Sarnaik 2021), pooled efficacy/safety (Chesney 2022), and FDA approval Feb 2024. Single-cell TCR-seq becomes routine. The lifileucel benchmark — 5.9% CR rate — is the regulatory comparator for any new ACT in melanoma. NCI Surgery Branch publishes their academic-best-case 24% CR rate for context.
- **Top:** PMID [33979178](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33979178/) — Sarnaik 2021 *JCO* — lifileucel C-144-01 pivotal.
- **Pooled:** PMID [36600653](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36600653/) — Chesney 2022 *JITC* — lifileucel efficacy/safety.

### Era 08 — Theodore-curated PDFs (55 papers)

The 55 papers (of 64 PDFs) Theodore had already pulled before this project started. Heavily weighted toward the corrected protocol family (vaccine + autologous tumor + BCG / VDLN harvest / ex-vivo activation) — these are the papers Theodore's instincts told him were closest to his program. This bucket validated the protocol-correction pivot mid-project.
- See `lit-corpus/by-era/08_theodore_curated.md` for the full list.

### Era 09 — Pan-cancer mechanism (175 papers)

Mechanistic papers from outside melanoma that inform the immunology — exhaustion biology, T-cell stem-like memory, antigen-presentation pathways, MHC class I/II processing. The methodological substrate for understanding why vaccine-priming and VDLN harvest might recruit a different lymphocyte population than tumor-resident TIL.

### Era 10 — Bridging era (2006–2014) — 297 papers

The decade that links the lymphodepletion era to the neoantigen era. Checkpoint inhibitors enter clinical use; ipilimumab approved 2011; pembrolizumab 2014. Whole-exome sequencing becomes affordable. Tumor mutational burden (TMB) emerges as biomarker. Most papers in this bucket are checkpoint-mechanism + TMB-as-biomarker work that sets up the neoantigen-discovery era.

### Era 11 — Lifileucel + KEYNOTE-942 pivotal (300 papers)

The largest pivotal-trial cluster. Includes lifileucel C-144-01 reports, follow-on trials, KEYNOTE-942 mRNA-4157, and the regulatory / payer / health-economics literature surrounding them. This is the modern competitive landscape against which the Logan protocol must be positioned.
- **Top:** PMID [33979178](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33979178/) — Sarnaik 2021 *JCO* — lifileucel pivotal.
- **mRNA-4157 era:** Long-term KEYNOTE-942 follow-up (multiple PMIDs in the bucket).

### Era 12 — Top authors 2018+ (300 papers)

Recent (2018+) work from the most-cited modern authors in the field — Rosenberg, Wu, Tran, Topalian, Schumacher, Sondel, Yee, Riddell, et al. Used during outreach planning to identify which collaborators are most productive in the modern era and which sub-disciplines they own.

### Era 13 — Grant infrastructure (300 papers)

### Era 16 — Chang / Shu lineage (182 papers)

Alfred Chang and Suyu Shu's vaccine-primed lymph node + anti-CD3 + IL-2 protocol family — the **direct published precedent** for the corrected Logan protocol. Chang/Yoshizawa/Sondak/Shu 1993 *Cancer Res* established the vaccine-primed T-lymphocyte adoptive-immunotherapy paradigm; Chang 1997 *JCO* and 2003 *JCO* are the clinical phase reports. This bucket is the lineage anchor for the entire revival proposal.
- **Top:** PMID [9053507](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9053507/) — Chang/Aruga/Sondak/Shu 1997 *JCO* — vaccine-primed lymph node cells + anti-CD3/IL-2 (★ direct VDLN precedent).
- **Phase II:** PMID [12610189](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12610189/) — Chang 2003 *JCO* — full vaccine + VDLN + anti-CD3 + IL-2 in RCC.

### Era 17 — Berd / DNP / M-Vax (143 papers)

David Berd and Michael Mastrangelo's DNP-modified autologous tumor vaccine (M-Vax) program at Thomas Jefferson — the closest historical commercial program in the same protocol family. M-Vax demonstrated long-term durable responses in melanoma but never achieved FDA approval. Important bucket for understanding the manufacturing / regulatory hurdles the Logan revival faces.
- **Top:** PMID [7884439](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7884439/) — Berd/Mastrangelo 1995 *JCO* — combination chemotherapy of metastatic melanoma (M-Vax era).
- **Mechanism:** PMID [7994925](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7994925/) — Sato/Maguire/Mastrangelo/Berd 1995 — DNP-conjugated vaccine immune response.

### Era 18 — Sondel anti-CD3 + IL-2 (113 papers)

Paul Sondel's translational immunology work, including the anti-CD3 + IL-2 ex-vivo activation step that became the UPCI-93-76 variant. Modern follow-on includes Sondel's neuroblastoma + anti-GD2 work. Sondel is a Tier-1 outreach target (LoS ask) for the proposal.
- **Top:** PMID [1834166](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1834166/) — Weil-Hillman/Sondel 1991 — anti-CD3 activation of human T cells obtained pre/post IL-2 therapy.

### Era 19 — Autologous tumor + BCG + VDLN (51 papers)

Targeted bucket for papers that combine *all three* protocol elements: autologous tumor antigen, BCG adjuvant, and lymph-node-derived effector cells. Smallest specific bucket because the protocol family is rare in the modern literature; most papers here are 1980s–1990s. This is the definitional bucket for the corrected Logan protocol.

### Era 20 — Hoover / Mukherji / Wallack historical (127 papers)

The earliest historical layer (1972–1995) — Herb Hoover's autologous-tumor + BCG colorectal trials, Bijay Mukherji's melanoma vaccine work, Marc Wallack's vaccinia-melanoma-oncolysate program. Pre-modern surgical-oncology immunotherapy. Important for establishing the deep historical precedent that the Logan revival sits in.
- **Top:** PMID [8445413](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8445413/) — Hoover/Brandhorst/Hanna 1993 *JCO* — adjuvant active specific immunotherapy for human colorectal cancer, 6.5-year median follow-up.
- **Hanna / vaccine framework:** PMID [7055781](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7055781/) — Hanna/Pollack/Hoover 1982 *Cancer* — active specific immunotherapy of micrometastases with BCG plus tumor cell vaccines.

## Cross-era themes

### Theme A — The TIL vs VDLN branch point

Two paradigms for sourcing therapeutic T cells:

1. **TIL paradigm** (Rosenberg, Iovance, NCI Surgery Branch): extract lymphocytes from resected tumor. The cells have already been exposed to tumor antigen but have also been exposed to the tumor microenvironment's exhaustion signals.
2. **VDLN paradigm** (Hoover-Berd-Chang-Sondel-Logan): vaccinate the patient with autologous tumor + BCG, then surgically harvest the *vaccine-draining lymph node*. The cells are antigen-experienced but have not yet trafficked to tumor — they are fresher, more stem-like, less exhausted.

Krishna 2020 (PMID 33303615) and Lowery 2022 (PMID 35113651) provide the modern evidence that stem-like, less-exhausted T cells are the responders. Krishna 2026 preprint (PMID 42052242) explicitly shows that *vaccination rescues dysfunctional ACT* — independent prospective validation that the VDLN branch was on the right track.

The Logan revival is the bet that vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T outperforms tumor-derived TIL on the stem-like-memory dimension.

### Theme B — The lymphodepletion question

TIL paradigm requires Cy/Flu lymphodepletion to make space for transferred cells. Lifileucel inherits this. The Logan protocol *deliberately omits* lymphodepletion — preserves the host immune environment so vaccine-amplified responses can persist. This is a meaningful protocol differentiator and a real question for the Phase 1 design: does the absence of lymphodepletion limit engraftment, or does it preserve a better post-transfer environment? The R21 mouse arm directly compares both conditions.

### Theme C — The IL-2 dose paradigm shift

High-dose IL-2 (720,000 IU/kg q8h) was the historical standard. It causes substantial toxicity (capillary leak syndrome, hypotension, cardiac stress). Modern protocols are shifting to lower doses. The Logan protocol uses 30,000 IU/kg q8h × 15 doses — substantially below high-dose, large therapeutic margin. Reviewer-cycle critique (Goff G2) acknowledged this as a meaningful modernization.

### Theme D — From bulk to single-cell

The 1990s field could only see bulk T-cell expansion (Weidmann/Logan 1993 used Vβ-PCR for oligoclonality detection). The 2026 field sees per-cell TCR α/β chains, transcriptomic state, and neoantigen-reactivity signatures. This is *the* methodological modernization the R21 + R01 capitalizes on. The same protocol that produced oligoclonal expansion 33 years ago can now be characterized at single-clone resolution to identify *which* clones drove response.

### Theme E — The combination question (vaccine + ACT + checkpoint)

The Krishna 2026 preprint suggests the optimal regimen may be vaccine *and* ACT *and* checkpoint inhibitor concurrently. The Wu reviewer cycle (W4) pushed for concurrent pembrolizumab in the R01 Phase 1 — adopted in the current draft. If the Phase 2 is positive, a follow-on combination R01 (vaccine + VDLN-IVS-T + anti-PD-1 + anti-LAG-3?) is the natural next step.

### Theme F — The durability puzzle

Lifileucel's 5.9% CR rate is low but the responders have multi-year durable disease control. NCI Surgery Branch's 24% CR rate has 5+ year median survival in responders. The unresolved question is what predicts who responds. Stem-like memory phenotype is the leading 2026 candidate. The Logan R01 Phase 2 will use Krishna 2020-style TCF1+ enrichment as a release criterion and longitudinal biomarker.
